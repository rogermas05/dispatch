The phone call becomes one more tool the agent can use, paid from its wallet.

Paid only if
it happens.

  1. QuotePriced by how long the call should take
  2. LockThe money sits in Masumi escrow before the phone rings
  3. CallDispatch makes the call and reports back
  4. ProveHashes of the brief and the result go on Cardano
  5. ReleaseThe seller is paid after the dispute window, or the buyer is refunded
